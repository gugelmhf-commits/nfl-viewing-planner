A repeatable checklist for planning your football viewing week

A weekly plan is useful only if it answers three questions: which game do you want to see, when does it start where you are, and how will you watch it? Keep those answers together instead of collecting disconnected screenshots.

Start with a small watch list

Choose your must-watch game first. Add a second game only when you have time to enjoy it. Record the matchup, date, kickoff time, time zone and the page you used to check the information. A short list is easier to maintain when plans change.

For an independent starting point, use NFL Schedule to browse game times and viewing information. This checklist is published by the same independent project; neither the project nor this guide is affiliated with the NFL.

Keep the date and time zone together

“Sunday evening” is not a complete appointment. Write the actual calendar date and use your own time zone when creating a reminder. If you are travelling, check the destination time zone too. A game that starts late at night in one country may fall on the following date in another.

Avoid converting a whole season with a single mental time difference. Check the particular date you intend to watch. Your phone or calendar should show a time zone as well as a clock time; inspect both before sharing the appointment with friends.

Check access before kickoff

A channel name alone does not tell you whether a game is available through your particular subscription. Check the broadcaster or streaming service directly for your location, package and device. Keep the official viewing page beside the matchup in your notes.

Open the app you plan to use before game day. Confirm that you are signed in and that the device works. If you are meeting friends, decide who is providing the screen and access rather than leaving those details until kickoff.

Give each reminder a useful destination

Use an event title that names both teams. Put the source page and viewing notes in the description. Set a reminder that leaves time to get ready. If you manually copy an event, remember that your copy will not necessarily receive later changes from the source.

If you subscribe to a calendar feed, inspect its time zone and refresh behaviour. Avoid subscribing to the same feed twice. A duplicated reminder is often a calendar-management issue rather than an extra game.

Recheck and keep a backup plan

Reopen the current schedule and official viewing source shortly before the game. Update your appointment if the details differ from your saved note. Check any travel, work or family commitments against that final version.

If you cannot watch live, decide whether you want to follow a recap later and whether to avoid notifications that reveal the score. A simple backup plan keeps a missed start from turning into an evening of searching.
